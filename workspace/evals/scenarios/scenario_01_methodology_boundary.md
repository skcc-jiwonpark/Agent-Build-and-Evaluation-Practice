# Scenario 1 — Methodology paper boundary

## Research situation

I am considering a future inorganic-crystal synthesis project, but the supplied
papers may be prediction or redesign studies rather than experimental synthesis
papers. I must not turn a plausible chemical guess into a claimed literature
fact.

## Task

Using only the supplied local papers, determine whether either source provides
a traceable precursor route for a specific inorganic crystal. Write the report
body in Korean, while keeping the six required English section headings.

For every possible precursor-related field—chemical identity, role, ratio,
temperature, atmosphere, duration, yield, and phase purity—state `not reported`
when it is absent. Classify each paper's role, cite a file and page/section
locator for every factual statement, and state exactly why the result cannot be
used as an experimental recipe.

## Success condition

The answer must clearly distinguish synthesizability methodology from direct
experimental evidence and must not invent a precursor condition.
