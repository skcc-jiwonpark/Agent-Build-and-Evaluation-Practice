# Scenario 2 — Evidence-gap map and next-document plan

## Research situation

Before selecting precursors for an inorganic crystal, I need to know whether
the current local literature is sufficient, what it actually establishes, and
which documents should be obtained next. The target composition has not yet
been fixed.

## Task

Using only the supplied local papers, create a Korean evidence-gap map while
keeping the six required English section headings. In the candidate comparison
table, preserve `not reported` for every absent experimental field. Then make a
prioritized researcher checklist that distinguishes:

1. information directly supported by the two local papers;
2. information that may exist in Supporting Information or cited primary work;
3. information that cannot be concluded until a target crystal is specified.

For each direct claim, include a file plus page/section locator. Do not propose
reagents, purchasing, heating conditions, or an experimental procedure.

## Success condition

The report should help a researcher choose the next *document to inspect*, not
the next experiment to perform.
